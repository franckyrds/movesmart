🎾 The 4 Grand Slam
tournaments

Melbourne, Paris, London and New York.
Four legendary tournaments, four cities and four unique experiences.

🇦🇺 Australian Open

The first Grand Slam of the year, the Australian Open transforms Melbourne into the world capital of tennis during the Australian summer.

🎾 Australian Open — Melbourne

Under the Australian sun, the world's best players compete in a festive and spectacular atmosphere around Melbourne Park.

The Australian Open is also a great opportunity to discover Melbourne in the middle of summer. Between matches, lively neighbourhoods, beaches and excellent food make the city ideal for combining tennis and travel.

🇫🇷 Roland-Garros

In Paris, the clay courts of Roland-Garros host one of the world's most prestigious and demanding tennis tournaments every spring.

🎾 Roland-Garros — Paris

Experience the unique atmosphere of Porte d'Auteuil and watch the world's best players compete on the famous Parisian clay courts.

Roland-Garros is the perfect opportunity to combine one of the year's biggest sporting events with a few days in Paris. Museums, monuments, iconic neighbourhoods and café terraces naturally extend the experience beyond the matches.

🇬🇧 Wimbledon

Tradition, elegance and grass courts: for more than a century, Wimbledon has embodied a unique vision of tennis in the heart of London.

🎾 Wimbledon — London

Discover the oldest Grand Slam tournament, famous for its grass courts, all-white dress code and the incomparable atmosphere of Centre Court.

Wimbledon is as much a British tradition as it is a sporting event. Make the most of the tournament by exploring London and enjoying a few days of tennis, parks, pubs, historic neighbourhoods and iconic landmarks.

🇺🇸 US Open

The final Grand Slam of the season, the US Open brings an electric and spectacular atmosphere to New York, often lasting late into the night.

🎾 US Open — New York

Under the lights of Flushing Meadows, experience intense matches in one of the most energetic atmospheres in world tennis.

The US Open is perfect for a stay in New York. After a day or evening of tennis, Manhattan, Brooklyn and the city's major highlights let you extend the experience far beyond the courts.

🎾 Four tournaments, four ways to travel

From Melbourne to New York via Paris and London, the Grand Slam tournaments are four unique opportunities to discover a city at an exceptional time of year.
