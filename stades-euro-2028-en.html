Euro 2028 Stadiums: All 9 Venues & 8 Host Cities

Discover stadium capacities, scheduled matches, transport connections and practical travel tips to plan your visit to UEFA Euro 2028.

🌍 Where Are the Euro 2028 Stadiums Located?

From 9 June to 9 July 2028, UEFA Euro 2028 will take place across 9 stadiums in 8 host cities: London, Manchester, Liverpool, Newcastle, Birmingham, Cardiff, Glasgow and Dublin. London is the only city hosting matches at two venues. Explore each stadium's capacity, key features and transport connections to plan your trip. 01
📍 London

Wembley Stadium

👥 88,000 seats ⚽ 8 matches 🏆 Final: 9 July 2028

Recognisable by its iconic arch, visible across much of north-west London, Wembley is one of the world's most famous football stadiums. Rebuilt and opened in 2007, it will host both semi-finals and the UEFA Euro 2028 final.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Wembley Park station (Jubilee and Metropolitan lines), Wembley Stadium station (Chiltern Railways) and Wembley Central (Bakerloo line and National Rail services) provide convenient access to the venue. From Wembley Park, visitors can walk along the famous pedestrian boulevard, Olympic Way.

Stations: Wembley Park · Wembley Stadium · Wembley Central

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches, 1 quarter-final, 2 semi-finals and the final.

Dates: 11, 14, 16 and 19 June; 30 June; 4, 5 and 9 July 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

For the final, staying near a direct Underground connection may be more convenient than booking a hotel immediately next to the stadium. Allow extra time after the match, as queues for public transport can be lengthy.

📍 London

Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

👥 60,000 seats ⚽ 5 matches 🏆 Round of 16: 26 June 2028

Opened in 2019 in north London, Tottenham Hotspur Stadium is one of Europe's most modern sports venues. Its innovative architecture and multi-purpose design make it an ideal setting for major international sporting events.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

White Hart Lane and Northumberland Park are the main railway stations serving the stadium. Tottenham Hale and Seven Sisters are also options, although they involve a longer walk or an additional connection.

Stations: White Hart Lane · Northumberland Park

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches and 1 round-of-16 match.

Dates: 12, 15, 18 and 21 June; 26 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Wembley and Tottenham are located in very different parts of London. Choose your accommodation based on the stadium listed on your ticket and the most convenient public transport connections.

📍 Manchester

Manchester City Stadium

👥 58,000 seats ⚽ 5 matches 🏆 Round of 16: 25 June 2028

Known as the Etihad Stadium for club competitions, Manchester City Stadium has hosted major football matches since 2003. Located east of Manchester city centre, the venue was notably the setting for the 2008 UEFA Cup Final.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

The Metrolink tram network serves Etihad Campus stop, just a short walk from the stadium. Connections are available from central Manchester, including via Piccadilly.

Transport: Metrolink · Etihad Campus

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches and 1 round-of-16 match.

Dates: 10, 13, 16 and 20 June; 25 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Consider staying in Manchester city centre or near a convenient tram connection. Check the services available after the final whistle and avoid relying entirely on travelling by car.

📍 Liverpool

Everton Stadium

👥 50,000 seats ⚽ 5 matches 🏆 Round of 16: 24 June 2028

Opened for the 2025–26 football season, Everton's new stadium is located at Bramley-Moore Dock on Liverpool's waterfront. This modern venue offers visitors an opportunity to discover part of the city's rich maritime heritage.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Sandhills station on the Merseyrail network is a recommended access point. From there, visitors can continue on foot or use dedicated transport arrangements where available for major events.

Transport: Merseyrail · Sandhills

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches and 1 round-of-16 match.

Dates: 11, 14, 17 and 20 June; 24 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Do not confuse this venue with Everton's former home, Goodison Park. Staying in Liverpool city centre can be a convenient option, provided you plan your return journey after the match.

📍 Newcastle

St James' Park

👥 50,000 seats ⚽ 5 matches 🏆 Round of 16: 25 June 2028

Located in the heart of Newcastle, St James' Park is one of English football's most historic stadiums. Its towering stands dominate the city skyline. The venue previously hosted matches during UEFA Euro 1996 and the London 2012 Olympic Games.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

The stadium is located next to St James Metro station. Monument station and Newcastle Central Station are also within walking distance, making it easy to travel without a car.

Stations: St James · Monument · Newcastle Central

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches and 1 round-of-16 match.

Dates: 12, 15, 17 and 20 June; 25 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

St James' Park is one of the easiest Euro 2028 venues to reach from a city centre. Accommodation near Newcastle Central Station allows you to explore the city and walk to the stadium on matchday.

📍 Birmingham

Villa Park

👥 48,000 seats ⚽ 4 matches 🏆 Round of 16: 27 June 2028

Villa Park is one of English football's most historic stadiums. Home to Aston Villa since 1897, the ground has a long tradition of hosting major domestic cup matches and is an important part of Birmingham's football heritage.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Witton railway station is approximately a five-minute walk from the stadium. Aston station is another option, around 15 minutes away on foot. Both stations are connected to Birmingham's rail network.

Transport: Train · Witton or Aston

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

3 group-stage matches and 1 round-of-16 match.

Dates: 11, 15 and 20 June; 27 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Staying near Birmingham New Street makes arriving by train and exploring the city centre easier. Stations near Villa Park can become particularly busy after matches, so allow extra time for your return journey.

📍 Cardiff

National Stadium of Wales

👥 73,000 seats ⚽ 6 matches 🏆 Opening Match: 9 June 2028

Also known as Principality Stadium, the National Stadium of Wales is located in the heart of Cardiff. Featuring a retractable roof, it will host the opening match of UEFA Euro 2028 as well as a quarter-final.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Cardiff Central, the city's main railway station, is just a short walk from the stadium. Road closures and designated pedestrian routes may be introduced on matchdays.

Transport: Train · Cardiff Central

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches, 1 round-of-16 match and 1 quarter-final.

Dates: 9, 14, 18 and 21 June; 24 June; 1 July 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Cardiff city centre is ideal for a stay without a car. Hotels near the railway station offer easy walking access to the stadium, although accommodation demand is likely to be high around the opening match.

📍 Glasgow

Hampden Park

👥 51,000 seats ⚽ 6 matches 🏆 Quarter-final: 1 July 2028

Scotland's national football stadium, Hampden Park has been the setting for many memorable European football occasions. It notably hosted the 2002 UEFA Champions League Final and several matches during UEFA Euro 2020.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Mount Florida and King's Park are the main railway stations serving the stadium. Both can be reached from Glasgow Central and are within walking distance of the venue.

Transport: Train · Mount Florida or King's Park

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

4 group-stage matches, 1 round-of-16 match and 1 quarter-final.

Dates: 10, 13, 17 and 21 June; 26 June; 1 July 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Staying near Glasgow Central makes it easier to use the rail network while exploring the city. Check for matchday traffic restrictions and allow extra time when leaving the stadium after the final whistle.

📍 Dublin

Dublin Arena

👥 50,000 seats ⚽ 7 matches 🏆 Quarter-final: 30 June 2028

Known locally as Aviva Stadium, Dublin Arena is located in the Lansdowne Road area of the Irish capital. This modern venue previously hosted the 2011 and 2024 UEFA Europa League finals.

🚉 Transport & Getting There

Lansdowne Road and Grand Canal Dock DART stations provide access to the stadium area. The most convenient route depends on the entrance indicated on your ticket and any matchday access arrangements.

Transport: DART · Lansdowne Road or Grand Canal Dock

📅 Euro 2028 Matches

5 group-stage matches, 1 round-of-16 match and 1 quarter-final.

Dates: 10, 12, 16, 19 and 21 June; 27 and 30 June 2028.

💡 Matchday Tip

Choose accommodation near a DART station or in the south-eastern part of Dublin city centre. Check the entrance shown on your ticket before deciding which station to use on matchday.

🧭 Plan Your Euro 2028 Trip

Choosing the right accommodation for UEFA Euro 2028 is not just about staying close to the stadium. Easy access to a railway station, Underground line or tram stop can make your matchday experience much more convenient.

🚆 Use Public Transport

Road access and traffic arrangements may change on matchdays. Check return transport schedules, use public transport where possible and allow extra time after the final whistle.

📍 Choose the Right Base City

In London, Wembley and Tottenham have different transport connections. Cardiff and Newcastle offer centrally located stadiums, while Manchester, Birmingham and Glasgow have useful rail and tram links. Choose your base according to the matches you plan to attend.

🛂 Check Entry Requirements

Entry requirements depend on your nationality and destination. Many international visitors to the United Kingdom need a valid passport and an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), unless exempt or required to obtain a visa. The Republic of Ireland has separate entry rules. EU citizens can generally enter Ireland with a valid passport or national identity card.

❓ FAQ: Euro 2028 Stadiums & Venues

How many stadiums will host Euro 2028?

Nine stadiums across eight host cities will stage UEFA Euro 2028 matches. London is the only city with two venues: Wembley Stadium and Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

What is the largest stadium at Euro 2028?

Wembley Stadium in London is the largest of the nine venues, with a UEFA-listed tournament capacity of 88,000 spectators, according to the September 2026 stadium guide.

Where will the Euro 2028 final take place?

The UEFA Euro 2028 final will take place at Wembley Stadium in London on 9 July 2028. Both semi-finals will also be played there on 4 and 5 July.

Which stadium will host the Euro 2028 opening match?

The National Stadium of Wales, also known as Principality Stadium, in Cardiff will host the opening match on 9 June 2028.

Which stadiums will host the Euro 2028 quarter-finals?

The four quarter-finals are scheduled to take place at Wembley Stadium in London, Dublin Arena, Hampden Park in Glasgow and the National Stadium of Wales in Cardiff.

Which Euro 2028 stadiums are close to railway stations?

Cardiff's stadium is within walking distance of Cardiff Central. St James' Park is next to St James Metro station, Villa Park is close to Witton railway station, and Wembley is served by several stations. Each venue has its own public transport connections.

Which teams will play at each Euro 2028 stadium?

The match schedule and stadium allocations have been announced, but the teams playing at each venue are not yet fully confirmed. They will depend on qualification results and the final tournament draw.

Do stadium capacities equal the number of tickets available?

No. The published capacities are UEFA reference figures for each venue. The actual number of tickets available to supporters may be lower depending on stadium configurations, technical installations and ticket allocations.

Do international visitors need a UK ETA for Euro 2028?

Many international visitors travelling to the United Kingdom need a valid passport and a UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA), unless exempt or required to obtain a visa. Requirements depend on nationality and immigration status. The Republic of Ireland has separate entry rules. Always check the latest official requirements before travelling.
