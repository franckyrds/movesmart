✨ New Sports & Major Returns at LA28

🏈 Flag Football

American football comes to the Olympic Games in a new format. Fast-paced and non-contact, flag football will make its Olympic debut in Los Angeles.

🎾 Squash

After years of anticipation, squash will finally join the Olympic programme. LA28 will crown the sport's first-ever Olympic champions.

🏏 Cricket

More than a century after its only previous Olympic appearance, cricket will return to the Games in Los Angeles in the fast-paced and spectacular T20 format.

🥍 Lacrosse

Lacrosse will also make its Olympic return with the Sixes format, a faster and more dynamic version of this historic North American sport.
