🌍 Where is Euro 2028 being held?

UEFA Euro 2028 will take place from 9 June to 9 July 2028 across England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland. The tournament will feature 24 teams and 51 matches played across 9 stadiums in 8 host cities.

The host cities are London, Manchester, Liverpool, Newcastle, Birmingham, Cardiff, Glasgow and Dublin. The opening match will be played at the National Stadium of Wales in Cardiff on 9 June 2028, while the semi-finals and final will take place at Wembley Stadium in London, with the final scheduled for 9 July 2028.
