🌍 Where Will UEFA Euro 2028 Take Place?

Get ready to experience one of Europe's biggest sporting events across some of the most iconic destinations in the United Kingdom and Ireland. UEFA Euro 2028 will bring together world-class football, legendary stadiums and exciting cities for an unforgettable tournament.

UEFA Euro 2028 will take place from 9 June to 9 July 2028 across England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland. The tournament will feature 24 teams playing 51 matches at 9 stadiums across 8 host cities.

From the energy of London and Manchester to the atmosphere of Glasgow, Cardiff and Dublin, each host city will offer a unique experience for football fans. The opening match will take place at the National Stadium of Wales in Cardiff on 9 June 2028, while both semi-finals and the final will be played at Wembley Stadium in London. The final is scheduled for 9 July 2028.
