🏁 Experience a Grand Prix Weekend

🏎️ Plan for several days: a Formula 1 weekend generally runs from Friday to Sunday, with practice sessions, qualifying and the Grand Prix.

🎟️ Book in advance: for the most popular Grands Prix, tickets and accommodation can be in high demand several months before the race.

🚆 Plan your journey to the circuit: some circuits are located outside city centres. Check the shuttle buses, trains and other transport services available during the Grand Prix weekend.

🏨 Choose your accommodation: staying in the main city often allows you to enjoy the Grand Prix atmosphere while also exploring the destination.

⏰ Arrive early: race days attract large crowds, and security checks and travel around the circuit can take extra time.

❓ Formula 1 FAQ

🏎️ How long does a Formula 1 weekend last?

A Grand Prix usually takes place over three days, from Friday to Sunday. The schedule generally includes practice sessions, qualifying and the race, although the format can vary during Sprint weekends.

🎟️ When should I book tickets for a Grand Prix?

It is best to book as early as possible, especially for the most popular Grands Prix. The best seating categories and accommodation close to the circuit can be booked several months in advance.

📅 Can I buy a ticket only for Friday, Saturday or Sunday?

This depends on the Grand Prix and the ticketing options offered by the organiser. Some circuits sell single-day tickets, while others mainly offer packages covering several days of the race weekend.

🚆 How do I get to a Formula 1 circuit?

Access varies greatly depending on the destination. Some circuits are close to a major city or accessible by public transport, while others may require a train, shuttle bus, car or dedicated transfer. Always check the transport options before travelling.

📆 Can the Formula 1 calendar change?

Yes. Dates, schedules or individual rounds can change during the season. Before booking your trip, always check the latest information published by Formula 1 and the organiser of the relevant Grand Prix.
