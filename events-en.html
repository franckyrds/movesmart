📅 January to March 2027

During the rainy season, a thin layer of water transforms the world's largest salt flat into an immense mirror where the sky and horizon seem to merge.

📍 Uyuni — Bolivia

How do I choose an event to plan my trip around?

Start by choosing the experience you want to enjoy, then check the dates, destination and best time to travel. A major event can become the starting point of a trip and a great opportunity to discover a city or region in a different way.

Are the events updated every year?

Yes. Dates, programmes and practical information may change from one edition to the next. Content is updated progressively to help you plan your trip at the right time.

Can I discover the destinations connected to an event?

Yes. Events are gradually linked to the relevant destination guides so you can discover the highlights, plan your stay and continue the experience beyond the event itself.
