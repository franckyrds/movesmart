Privacy Policy

Vidolta places particular importance on protecting personal data and user privacy.

🔒 Personal data

Vidolta does not directly collect sensitive personal data when users simply browse the website.

Certain statistical and technical information may nevertheless be available through the services used by Vidolta, particularly to monitor the website's visibility in search engines and to improve its content and performance.

📊 Browsing data and statistics

Vidolta uses Google Search Console to monitor the website's visibility and performance in Google search results.

This service provides aggregated statistical information including:

• search queries
• impressions and clicks
• pages appearing in search results
• device types
• countries of origin
• overall performance in search results

This information is used to better understand Vidolta's visibility in search engines, improve the content provided and optimize the website's performance.

🍪 Cookies

Vidolta does not currently use advertising cookies or audience measurement cookies directly, such as those that may be associated with Google Analytics.

Certain services or partner websites accessible from Vidolta may, however, use their own cookies, identifiers or similar technologies.

When a user leaves Vidolta to access an external service, any use of cookies is subject to the terms and privacy policy of the service concerned.

📈 Performance analysis

Vidolta uses Google Search Console to analyze the website's performance in Google search results.

The available data includes, in particular, clicks, impressions, search queries, the pages concerned, device types and the countries from which searches are performed.

This information is mainly used for statistical and technical purposes and to improve Vidolta's content.

Vidolta does not currently use Google Analytics or any similar solution to track visitor behavior in detail directly on the website.

🤝 Affiliate links and partners

Some Vidolta pages contain affiliate links to booking, activity, ticketing or travel platforms.

When a user makes a booking or purchase after clicking one of these links, Vidolta may receive a commission at no additional cost to the user.

These commissions help support the website and the development of new content.

Vidolta may work with platforms such as Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Hellotickets or other travel, ticketing and booking services.

The recommendations and content published on Vidolta remain independent of these commercial partnerships.

When a user clicks on an affiliate link, the relevant partner may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to identify the origin of the click and attribute a potential booking or transaction to Vidolta.

Any data processing carried out after accessing a partner website is subject to that partner's terms and privacy policy.

🔗 Third-party services

Vidolta may provide links to third-party websites, platforms or services.

When a user leaves Vidolta to access an external service, any data processing carried out on that service is the responsibility of the relevant third party and is governed by its own privacy policy.

Vidolta encourages users to review the privacy policies of the external services they use.

🛡️ Security

Vidolta implements reasonable measures to protect information and secure access to the website.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

⚖️ Your rights

In accordance with applicable personal data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), individuals may, where these rights apply, request access to, rectification or deletion of their personal data, as well as restriction of or objection to certain types of processing.
