Privacy Policy

Vidolta places great importance on protecting personal data and ensuring user privacy.

🔒 Personal Data

Vidolta respects the privacy of its users and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data through the website.

Certain technical information may nevertheless be collected automatically when browsing the website in order to ensure its proper operation, measure its audience and improve the user experience.

Vidolta does not sell users' personal data.

📊 Browsing Data

Certain browsing and technical data may be collected to understand how the website is used, monitor its performance and improve its content and navigation.

This information may include:

• device type
• operating system
• browser used
• pages visited
• date and duration of visits
• approximate geographic location
• general website usage statistics

Whenever possible, this information is analyzed in an aggregated or anonymized form.

🍪 Cookies

Vidolta and certain third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for the operation of the website, audience measurement or the attribution of affiliate transactions.

Depending on the technologies used, certain cookies may require the user's consent before being placed on their device.

Users may also configure their browser to block, delete or restrict cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of some website features.

📈 Analytics

Vidolta uses website performance and analytics tools to better understand its visibility in search engines and to improve its content, performance and user experience.

Vidolta currently uses Google Search Console, which provides aggregated statistical information such as:

• search queries
• impressions and clicks
• pages appearing in search results
• device types
• countries of origin
• general search performance statistics

This information is used primarily for statistical, technical and website improvement purposes.

Vidolta does not currently use Google Analytics or similar audience-tracking tools directly on the website.

🤝 Affiliate Links & Partners

Some pages on Vidolta contain affiliate links to travel, accommodation, ticketing, activity or booking platforms.

When a user clicks an affiliate link and subsequently makes a booking or purchase, Vidolta may receive a commission at no additional cost to the user.

These commissions help finance the operation of the website and the creation of new travel content.

Vidolta may work with partners such as Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Hellotickets and other travel, ticketing or booking platforms.

The presence of an affiliate partnership does not determine the editorial content published on Vidolta. Recommendations, destination information and travel content remain independently selected and presented.

Affiliate partners or affiliate networks may use cookies, tracking links or similar technologies to identify the origin of a booking or purchase and attribute the corresponding commission.

When users leave Vidolta and access a partner website, the processing of their personal data is governed by the privacy policy and terms of that third-party service.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Vidolta may contain links to external websites, booking platforms, ticket providers, tourism services and other third-party services.

These websites and services operate independently and apply their own privacy policies, cookie policies and terms of use.

Vidolta does not control the data processing practices of these third-party services and cannot be held responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party services before providing personal information or completing a transaction.

🛡️ Data Security

Vidolta takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the website and any information processed through it against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no website, internet transmission or online storage system can guarantee absolute security.
