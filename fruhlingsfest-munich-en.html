🍺 What is Frühlingsfest Munich?

Frühlingsfest Munich 2027, commonly known in English as Munich Springfest or the Munich Spring Festival, is one of Bavaria's most popular spring celebrations. It is often described as the city's “Little Oktoberfest”.

Held at the famous Theresienwiese, the festival brings together traditional beer tents, Bavarian food, fairground rides, family attractions and a lively local atmosphere.

The 2027 edition is scheduled to take place from April 16 to May 2, 2027. Admission to the festival grounds is free, while rides, food and drinks are paid separately.

📅 Frühlingsfest Munich 2027 Dates & Location

Everything you need to know about when and where Frühlingsfest takes place, and how to reach the Theresienwiese.

📅

Dates

Friday, April 16 to Sunday, May 2, 2027

Frühlingsfest runs across three weekends. Programmes and special-event times may still be updated before the festival.

📍

Location

Theresienwiese, Munich

The festival takes place on Munich's famous Theresienwiese, the same grounds that host Oktoberfest each autumn.

💡 Public transport is generally the easiest option, especially during busy evenings and weekends.

🎺 The Bavarian Tradition Behind Frühlingsfest

Frühlingsfest is more than a fairground: it is part of Munich's living festival culture and marks the beginning of Bavaria's traditional outdoor celebration season.

📜 A Munich Tradition Since 1965

Munich's Spring Festival was first held at the Theresienwiese in 1965.

It was created to give local showmen and fairground businesses their first major opportunity to work again after the quieter winter months.

🍺 Why “Little Oktoberfest”?

Frühlingsfest takes place on the same grounds as Oktoberfest and shares several of its traditions, including the brewery procession, ceremonial beer-barrel tapping, festival tents and Bavarian music.

The spring event is smaller and usually offers a more relaxed and locally focused atmosphere.

🎶 Music, Clothing and Customs

Brass bands, traditional songs and Bavarian dances form an important part of the festival programme.

Some visitors wear a dirndl or lederhosen, especially during celebrations in the festival tents, although traditional clothing is never required.

🎉 Frühlingsfest Munich 2027 Programme

From the traditional opening ceremony to family days, historic vehicles and Bavarian celebrations, Frühlingsfest offers special events throughout the festival. Some dates and timings may still be updated before April 2027.

🍺 Friday, April 16

Opening Day

From 3 pm, breweries and showmen traditionally make their ceremonial procession onto the Theresienwiese.

At around 4 pm, the festival officially begins with the traditional beer-barrel tapping inside the Hippodrom festival tent.

🛍️ Saturday, April 17

Traditional Flea Market

The large Bavarian Red Cross flea market is scheduled to begin from 7 am at the Theresienwiese.

It is traditionally one of the largest flea markets in southern Germany, bringing hundreds of stalls to the festival grounds.

🚗 Sunday, April 18

Vintage Car Meeting

A traditional gathering of historic vehicles is expected near the Bavaria statue.

The event usually combines a morning procession with a public display of classic and vintage vehicles.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 April 20 & 27

Family Days

The two Tuesdays are scheduled as special family days, traditionally running from around noon until 7 pm.

Reduced prices are usually available on selected rides, attractions and food offers, making these particularly good days for families.

🎆 Festival Fireworks

Evenings at the Theresienwiese

Fireworks are a traditional highlight of Frühlingsfest, with two displays usually organised during the festival.

The exact dates and times for the 2027 fireworks may be confirmed closer to the event.

🎺 Sunday, May 2

Day of Customs

The final Sunday traditionally celebrates Bavarian culture, with festivities scheduled to begin from around 11 am.

Traditional music, regional groups, costumes and dances bring the festival to a distinctly Bavarian conclusion.

🕒 Frühlingsfest 2027 Opening Hours

Monday to Friday: 11 am to 11 pm.

Saturday and Sunday: 10 am to 11 pm.

Festival tents are scheduled to remain open until 11 pm, with last drinks served at approximately 10:30 pm.

💡 Good to know: Opening hours, special events and scheduled activities may still be updated before the festival begins.

💡 Practical Tips for Visiting Frühlingsfest

A little preparation can make your visit to Munich Springfest more comfortable, especially during busy evenings, weekends and special programme days.

🕰️ Choose the Right Time

Weekday afternoons generally offer a calmer atmosphere and can be more convenient for exploring the rides, food stalls and festival grounds.

Friday evenings and weekends are usually livelier and may be more crowded.

🍺 Consider a Tent Reservation

Visitors can usually enter the festival tents without a reservation when space is available.

Booking a table can still be useful for groups or anyone planning to visit during a busy evening or weekend.

🌦️ Prepare for Spring Weather

Munich's weather can be changeable in April and early May, with mild sunshine, cooler evenings and occasional rain.

Bring an extra layer and lightweight rain protection, particularly if you plan to remain at the festival after sunset.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Visit on Family Days

Families should consider visiting on April 20 or April 27, 2027, when selected rides, attractions and food offers are expected to be available at reduced prices.

Arriving earlier in the day can make the experience easier with younger children.

💶 Keep More Than One Payment Option

Card payments may be available in many locations, but carrying some euros in cash can still be useful for smaller stalls, rides and quick purchases.

Prices vary between attractions, food stands and festival tents.

👗 Traditional Clothing Is Optional

Some visitors wear a dirndl or lederhosen, particularly during evenings and traditional programme events.

Traditional clothing is not required. Comfortable shoes are more important, as visitors may spend several hours walking and standing.

🎡 What to Expect at Munich Springfest

Frühlingsfest combines Bavarian traditions with the colourful atmosphere of a large fairground, offering a more relaxed spring alternative to Oktoberfest.

🍺 Traditional Beer Tents

Large festival tents welcome visitors with Bavarian beer, regional dishes, live music and the friendly atmosphere associated with Munich's traditional celebrations.

Entry to the tents is generally free, but reserving a table can be useful for groups or visitors planning to attend during busy evenings and weekends.

🎢 Rides and Attractions

The Theresienwiese becomes a colourful fairground filled with classic carousels, family attractions, games and more thrilling rides.

Individual rides and attractions require payment, while visitors can walk through the festival grounds freely.

🥨 Bavarian Food

Visitors can enjoy traditional festival favourites such as pretzels, sausages, roast chicken, sweet treats and other Bavarian specialities.

Food stalls and festival tents offer options throughout the day, making Frühlingsfest an easy place to stop for lunch, dinner or an evening drink.

🎶 A More Local Atmosphere

Often called Munich's “Little Oktoberfest”, Frühlingsfest offers many of the same traditions in a smaller and generally more relaxed setting.

Traditional Bavarian clothing is welcome but not required. Visitors can simply come as they are and enjoy the music, attractions and festive atmosphere.

❓ Frühlingsfest Munich 2027 FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions about Munich Springfest 2027, including dates, admission, opening hours, transport and reservations.

What are the Frühlingsfest Munich 2027 dates?

Frühlingsfest Munich 2027 is currently scheduled to take place from Friday, April 16 to Sunday, May 2, 2027, across three weekends.

Where is Munich Springfest 2027 held?

Munich Springfest takes place at the Theresienwiese, the same large festival grounds used for Oktoberfest.

Is admission to Frühlingsfest Munich free?

Yes. Entry to the festival grounds and festival tents is generally free. Visitors pay separately for rides, attractions, food and drinks.

What are the Frühlingsfest 2027 opening hours?

The published opening hours are 11 am to 11 pm from Monday to Friday and 10 am to 11 pm on Saturdays and Sundays. Festival tents serve their final drinks at approximately 10:30 pm.

How do I get to Frühlingsfest Munich?

The easiest route is the U4 or U5 underground line to Theresienwiese. Visitors can also use the S-Bahn to Hackerbrücke or the U3 and U6 lines to Goetheplatz, followed by a walk of approximately 10 minutes.

Do I need to reserve a table in a beer tent?

A reservation is not required to enter Frühlingsfest. However, reserving a table can be useful for groups and visitors attending the beer tents during busy evenings or weekends.

Is Munich Spring Festival suitable for families?

Yes. Frühlingsfest includes family rides, traditional attractions, games and food stalls. Family Days are scheduled for April 20 and April 27, 2027, with reduced prices traditionally available on selected attractions and food offers.

What is the difference between Frühlingsfest and Oktoberfest?

Both festivals take place at the Theresienwiese and feature beer tents, Bavarian food, music and fairground rides. Frühlingsfest is held in spring, is smaller than Oktoberfest and generally offers a more relaxed and locally focused atmosphere.
